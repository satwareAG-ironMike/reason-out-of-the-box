## Relevance to the core question

The anchor cites this patient as a case where consciousness is retained without a
cerebellum, which is a reason against structure-function inference (a C1/C3 case
rather than evidence against AI consciousness). A brain without a cerebellum is a
strong counterexample to structure-function inference, but it also raises the question
of which structure matters.

## Limitations and counterarguments

A single case report cannot settle the relevance of a structure. The anchor uses it to
show a structure is not necessary for behaviour, not to show consciousness can exist
without any structure, and the letter does not provide a direct measure of the
patient's consciousness.
