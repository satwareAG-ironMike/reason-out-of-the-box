## Abstract (condensed)
Intervenes on CoT: truncating steps, inserting mistakes, paraphrasing, adding filler. Measures
whether answer probabilities change accordingly. Faithfulness varies strongly by task and
often decreases with model scale.

## Key findings
- On some tasks CoT is causally load-bearing (corrupting a step flips the answer); on others
  the answer is fixed before the explanation begins.
- Scaling makes CoT *less* faithful on some tasks - larger models can afford to skip
  stating the real cause.

## Relevance to core question
**Calibration tool**: CoT faithfulness is real but partial and task-dependent. Any evaluation
of "LLM reasoning" must include faithfulness probes, because the model's narrative is not
automatically its mechanism.
