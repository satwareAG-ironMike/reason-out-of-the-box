## Abstract (condensed)
Addresses the disagreement over LLM creativity (superhuman vs fixation/homogenization
findings) by isolating one measurable failure mode: design fixation (anchoring to example
ideas). Provides a goal-oriented evaluation framework for defixation prompting.

## Key findings
- Much of the LLM-creativity controversy is a measurement problem: narrow decontextualized
  tasks inflate scores; broad tasks confound quality, originality, and fixation.
- Fixation is real and prompt-addressable: structured "defixation" prompting measurably
  reduces anchoring to example solutions.

## Relevance to core question
**Measurement tool for uniqueness**: before claiming LLMs do or do not produce unique
ideas, control fixation to example ideas - a large share of "novel" LLM output is
recombined anchoring. Gives the archive a benchmark-ability criterion for future claims.
