## Abstract (condensed)
Largest head-to-head divergent-creativity comparison: humans (N = 9,198) vs LLMs
(N = 215,542 observations) on established creativity tasks. Human average creativity is
slightly higher than LLM average; analysis covers originality distributions and
model-vs-model variation.

## Key findings
- Human average edges out LLM average on divergent-thinking tasks - contradicts the
  "LLMs beat humans on creativity tests" readings from smaller earlier samples
  (e.g., 1%-percentile AUT claims of 2023).
- Scale of both samples removes the small-N artifact risk that plagued 2023-2024
  comparisons.

## Relevance to core question
**Population-level calibration for the uniqueness question**: LLMs sit close to, slightly
below, average human divergent creativity - they produce unique ideas at roughly average
human rates, not superhuman rates, with heavy overlap between distributions.
