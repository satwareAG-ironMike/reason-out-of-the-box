## Abstract (condensed)
Longitudinal analysis of LLM creative outputs across three years of model releases on
open-ended real-world queries (Infinity-Chat100) and Alternative Uses-style tasks,
measuring quality, originality, and diversity.

## Key findings
- Tracks the "artificial hivemind" trend: different model families converge toward
  similar outputs on open-ended tasks - originality and diversity metrics evolve
  differently from quality metrics.
- Documents whether newer models are more creative or merely more polished and more alike.

## Relevance to core question
**The homogenization angle on uniqueness**: an idea is only "unique" relative to the space
of produced ideas. If models converge on the same ideas, individual novelty claims lose
meaning - uniqueness must be measured against the population of generated ideas, not per
output.
