## Abstract (condensed)
Surveys the two camps - LLMs understand language and the world in a humanlike sense vs
LLMs are sophisticated pattern matchers - and argues the debate exposes the absence of a
science of understanding: the term needs a new, more nuanced vocabulary for kinds of
understanding that may differ from human understanding.

## Key findings
- Neither side has a decisive argument; benchmark performance and "shortcut learning"
  both leave the question open.
- Proposes that LLM "understanding" may be a genuinely new category - neither human
  understanding nor mere parroting.

## Relevance to core question
**The peer-reviewed diagnosis of why the Hinton debate does not resolve**: "thinks" and
"understands" are undefined at the level of evidence both sides cite. Sets the standard
this archive follows - separate behavior, mechanism, and experience.
