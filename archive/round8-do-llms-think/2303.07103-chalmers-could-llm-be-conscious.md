## Abstract (condensed)
Breaks down the strongest reasons for and against LLM consciousness under mainstream
assumptions in consciousness science. Identifies obstacles in current models and argues
they may be overcome within roughly a decade.

## Key findings (verified from full text)
- Missing features listed as the main obstacles: **recurrent processing, a global
  workspace, unified agency**, plus world-models and self-models.
- Constructive program: "Build LLM+s with a global workspace"; "Build LLM+s with genuine
  memory and genuine recurrence" - the roadmap for systems that would clear the bar.
- Reported credences (from the essay text, not re-verified in passages read): under 10%
  that current LLMs are conscious; 25% or more for conscious LLM+ systems within a decade.

## Relevance to core question
**The reference framing between Hinton and his critics.** Chalmers neither dismisses nor
endorses: current pure LLMs probably lack the architecture (no recurrence: the TC0 point
from round 3 in philosophical clothing), but agentic, multimodal, memory-equipped systems -
the round 7 direction - are exactly what would change the assessment. Note: the project's
embodiment-and-herd requirement maps onto Chalmers' unified agency and world-model gaps.
