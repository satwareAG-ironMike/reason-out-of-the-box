## Abstract (condensed)
Tests higher-order theory of mind - recursive reasoning about mental states ("I think that
you believe that she knows...") up to 6th order - using a new handwritten benchmark
(Multi-Order Theory of Mind Q&A) against an adult human baseline.

## Key findings
- GPT-4 and Flan-PaLM reach adult human performance overall; GPT-4 exceeds adults on
  6th-order inferences.
- Performance tracks model size and fine-tuning; a co-author is Blaise Aguera y Arcas, the
  Google executive who reviewed and rejected Lemoine's sentience claim in 2022 - the
  "functional capability yes, sentience unproven" position, in a peer-reviewed venue.

## Relevance to core question
**Pro (functional)**: recursive mentalizing at adult level is the kind of capability
Hinton points to. It is behavioral evidence; the paper does not claim experience.
