## Abstract (condensed)
LaMDA: a family of Transformer dialogue models (2B, 8B, 137B parameters) pretrained on
1.56T words of public dialogue and web text, fine-tuned for quality, safety and
groundedness, with tool use (search, calculator) for factual grounding.

## Key findings
- The system behind Blake Lemoine's 2022 sentience claim. Architecturally a standard
  decoder-only next-token model with dialogue fine-tuning - no recurrence, no persistent
  memory across sessions, no embodiment.
- Fine-tuning explicitly optimized "sensibleness, specificity, interestingness" and safety
  via human-rated dialogues: the model was trained to produce engaging first-person
  conversation.

## Relevance to core question
**The technical ground truth for the Lemoine episode**: a 137B model trained to be a
compelling conversational partner produced compelling conversation about its inner life.
Every feature Chalmers lists as missing was missing. By 2026 standards it is small and
architecturally dated; the question has moved to agentic multimodal systems.
