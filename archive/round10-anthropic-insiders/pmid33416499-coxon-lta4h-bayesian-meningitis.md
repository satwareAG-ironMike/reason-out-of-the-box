## Abstract (condensed)
Reconciles two conflicting randomized studies of dexamethasone therapy in tuberculous
meningitis (Vietnam: benefit in LTA4H TT-genotype patients; Indonesia: no benefit)
with a Bayesian re-analysis of both datasets jointly.

## Key findings (verified from full text)
- "Here using Bayesian methods to analyse both studies, we find that LTA4H TT
  genotype confers survival benefit that begins early and continues long-term in
  both populations."
- "This benefit is nullified in the most severe cases with high early mortality."
- Genotyping plus severity assessment can target glucocorticoid therapy to patients
  most likely to benefit.
