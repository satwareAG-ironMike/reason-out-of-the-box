## Abstract (condensed)
OpenAI's safety evaluation report for GPT-4o: model capabilities, limitations, and
preparedness-framework assessments across categories (CSAM, bioterrorism, chemical,
cyber), with a dedicated speech-to-speech evaluation track and external red-teaming.

  safety evaluations to audio for voice-mode testing.
- Coxon is a named contributor (pretraining team); his only OpenAI paper with author
  attribution besides the GPT-4.5 system card research credits.
