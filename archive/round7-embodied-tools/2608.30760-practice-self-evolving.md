## Abstract (condensed)
Argues existing experience-based agent methods rely on manually designed prompting
workflows to extract and update skills, which fail to adapt to new and diverse situations.
PRACTICE learns the experience-to-skill conversion itself, making agents self-evolving.

## Key findings
- Automates the "reflect and store" loop: experience summarization, skill extraction, and
  library updates are learned rather than hard-coded.
- Targets continual improvement under distribution shift in embodied settings.

## Relevance to core question
**Removing the human from the learning loop**: the last hand-crafted piece of the
brain-body loop (how to digest experience) becomes learned - pushing the
"stored learnings" mechanism toward autonomy.
