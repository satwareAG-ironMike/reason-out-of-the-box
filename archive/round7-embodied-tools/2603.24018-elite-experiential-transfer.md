## Abstract (condensed)
Identifies the gap between VLM static training data and physical interaction: embodied
agents skip steps, propose invalid actions, and repeat mistakes. ELITE adds experiential
learning (from own interaction trajectories) and intent-aware transfer to convert failures
into reusable competence.

## Key findings
- Failure trajectories, properly curated, become the training signal for avoiding repeats -
  mistakes as first-class learning data.
- Transfer across tasks guided by intent matching, not surface similarity.

## Relevance to core question
**Error-driven learning loop for agents**: the missing piece in round 5 (LLMs cannot
self-correct) is supplied by the environment - errors observed in the world get converted
into corrections the pure-LLM loop cannot produce by introspection.
