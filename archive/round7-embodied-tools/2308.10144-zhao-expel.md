## Abstract (condensed)
Agents gather experience across training tasks, then extract natural-language insights by
comparing successful and failed trajectories; insights and retrieved successful trajectories
are provided at inference for new tasks. No parameter updates; works with closed models.

## Key findings
- Cross-task insight extraction (rules like "always check X before Y") transfers to unseen
  tasks - abstraction over experience, not just replay.
- Insights are auditable text: the learned knowledge is readable, editable, versionable.

## Relevance to core question
**Git-as-memory, formalized**: experience -> distilled written insights -> reused across
tasks. The "store learnings" mechanism, shown to improve agents without training.
Limitation shared with Reflexion: insight quality depends on trustworthy success labels.
