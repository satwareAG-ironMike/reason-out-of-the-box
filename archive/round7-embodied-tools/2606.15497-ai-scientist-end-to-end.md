## Abstract (condensed)
Presents the strongest demonstration to date of automating the entire scientific process
end-to-end: the system creates research ideas, writes code, runs experiments, plots and
analyzes data, writes the full scientific manuscript, and performs its own peer review.

## Key findings
- The idea-generation step is inseparable from the **experimental loop**: ideas are ranked
  and revised through execution feedback (code runs, results, review scores).
- Known weaknesses carried through the lineage: generated papers contain citation errors,
  review self-assessment is unreliable, and reviewer-model agreement with humans is
  limited - validation remains the bottleneck, not ideation.
- v2 achieved workshop-level acceptance at ICLR 2025 workshop scale; headline claim is
  full-cycle automation, not superhuman creativity.

## Relevance to core question
**Direct embodiment test of the embodiment hypothesis at the research frontier**: give the LLM a
body (code execution, GPUs) and a validation loop (experiments + review) and it produces
novel work - at workshop tier, not above it. Confirms: tools extend capability; they do
not yet exceed trained-human creativity.
