## Abstract (condensed)
Asks whether the dynamics of visible chain of thought (sequencing, repetition, timing of
reasoning functions) can distinguish successful from failed reasoning without assuming
the trace is a faithful account of internal computation. On verifiable Boolean
satisfiability tasks with controlled complexity, incorrect traces show premature
verification collapse; a targeted proof-search prompt intervention lifts Llama3-70B from
13.3% to 85% on the diagnosed failure mode.

## Key findings (verified from full text)
- 5 LLM configurations on SAT/UNSAT instances at capability-frontier regimes (both
  correct and incorrect traces on comparable problems); CoT sentences tagged by
  reasoning function; features: role densities, transitions, cycles, entropy,
  finalization timing.
- SAT failures: premature verification collapse - incorrect traces enter clause
  checking earlier, repeat similar operations, and finalize sooner despite comparable
  clause coverage. Within-family comparison (Llama3-8B wrong vs Llama3-70B correct)
  preserves the repetition and entropy effects but not length and verification effects:
  a recurring pattern, not a universal signature.
- UNSAT failures: models presumptuously pursue SAT conclusions, checking candidate
  assignments rather than deriving contradictions across constructed cases.
- Early warning: many incorrect traces flaggable before answer emission, with
  model-specific thresholds fit on one half of problems and evaluated on the other.
- Intervention: targeted proof-search prompt (split into cases, propagate forced
  consequences, derive contradictions) raises Llama3-70B accuracy from 13.3% to 85%,
  correcting 84.6% of wrong-SAT failures.
- Stated limitations: intervention is post hoc and oracle-assisted (solver labels
  decide which failures are rerun); the generic-retry baseline is unmatched in length
  and detail; generality beyond formal tasks open.

## Relevance to core question
Complements the round 5 native-self-correction negative: failures are detectable from
trajectory structure rather than statement-by-statement semantics, but detection and the
fix are analysis plus prompting by the experimenters, not a model faculty. The diagnosis
itself (which procedure is missing) is externally verified against a solver. No claim
about untrained out-of-the-box reasoning; useful as a monitoring-method datapoint.
