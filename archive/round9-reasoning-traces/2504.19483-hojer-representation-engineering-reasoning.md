## Abstract (condensed)
Uses representation engineering to probe whether reasoning-relevant structure is present
in LLM activations: control vectors derived from residual-stream activations on reasoning
tasks are applied as inference-time interventions, improving reasoning performance on
inductive (IOI), deductive (bAbI), and mathematical (GSM8K) tasks across Pythia and
Mistral models.

## Key findings (verified from full text)
- Method: control vectors trained on activations from a reasoning-task training set,
  applied at the middle residual-stream layer at inference; no model weights are
  trained ("We do not train any models, but extract control vectors based on hidden
  states"). Vectors derived from the training set only, tested separately.
- Models: Pythia-1.4B, Pythia-2.8B (EleutherAI, open data lineage), Mistral-7B-Instruct.
- Results: generally improved performance across models and tasks, with task-dependent
  optimal intervention strength (alpha) and sign; the intervention mostly encodes
  position-sensitive information on IOI; entropy and KL-diagnostics confirm intended
  effects.
- Stated limitations: models smaller than production scale (largest 7B); IOI may be too
  simple to elicit a general representation; indirect contamination cannot be ruled out;
  derivation of optimal control vectors left to future work.
- Peer-review: ICLR 2025 (proceedings.iclr.cc 2025 paper file; official code repository).

## Relevance to core question
Peer-reviewed evidence that reasoning-relevant structure is latent in the residual
stream of open-data-lineage models (Pythia), but is only accessible through an
intervention derived from task data. Same pattern as the rest of round 9: latent and
extractable, not demonstrated out of the box. Directly usable as a related-method
citation for the thesis intervention arm.
