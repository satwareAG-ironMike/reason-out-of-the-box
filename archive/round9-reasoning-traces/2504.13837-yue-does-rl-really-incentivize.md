# Does Reinforcement Learning Really Incentivize Reasoning Capacity in LLMs Beyond the Base Model?

## Abstract (condensed)
Critically examines whether RL with verifiable rewards (RLVR) actually elicits novel
reasoning abilities beyond the base model. Comparing pass@k curves of base and RLVR
models across benchmarks and difficulty, RLVR models dominate at small k but base models
catch up and surpass them at large k, indicating RLVR narrows rather than extends the
boundary of solvable problems.

## Key findings (verified from full text)
- RLVR models: SimpleRLZoo (GRPO on GSM8K + MATH training sets, correctness-only
  reward, no format reward), plus Oat-Zero-7B and DAPO-32B; compared against their
  base models on GSM8K, MATH500, Minerva, OlympiadBench, AIME24, AMC23.
- Pass@k crossover: at k=1 (average accuracy) RL models win; as k grows, base models
  catch up and eventually surpass RL models on all benchmarks. On Minerva with a 32B
  model, the base outperforms the RL model by about 9% at k=128 (roughly 9% more
  problems covered). Oat-Zero: RL starts nearly 30% higher, is surpassed at large k.
- CoT validity control: for the hardest GSM8K problems (average accuracy below 5%),
  the base model answered 25 questions with 24 containing at least one manually
  verified correct CoT (RL-trained: 23 of 25) - the large-k gains are not lucky guesses.
- Authors' conclusion: RLVR increases the likelihood of sampling correct responses at
  low k but narrows overall coverage; reasoning paths are already present in base
  models ("Reasoning Paths Already Present in Base Models" analysis section).
- Peer-review: NeurIPS 2025 Oral (neurips.cc virtual 119944; OpenReview 4OsgYD7em5).

## Relevance to core question
The strongest peer-reviewed quantification of the elicitation-only reading: RL reshapes
sampling toward reward-compatible modes and reduces exploration, rather than creating
reasoning. Directly supports the archive verdict ("RL elicits and amplifies latent
competence") and bounds what the thesis H1 experiment should expect from RL baselines.
Still on undisclosed-data models, so contamination is not excluded.
