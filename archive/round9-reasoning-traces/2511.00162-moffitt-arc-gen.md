## Abstract (condensed)
Presents ARC-GEN, a procedural benchmark generator for the Abstraction and Reasoning
Corpus that produces mimetic examples per task, addressing the intra-task sample
scarcity of a suite that measures skill-acquisition efficiency rather than accumulated
knowledge or task-specific skill.

## Key findings
- Mimetic procedural generation of ARC task examples, targeted at algorithms that
  require extensive intra-task training data (abstract).
- Open-source implementation (verified 2026-09-03, docs/next-investigations.md);
  complements 2404.07353 (per-task generators for all 400 training tasks).

## Relevance to core question
Second reusable ARC generator: covers the task-family side of Condition 2 (procedural
novel items, held-out seeds, seed-hash verification). Preprint, tooling only; no
reasoning claim.
