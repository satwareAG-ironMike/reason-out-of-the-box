## Abstract (condensed)
Proposes WIMBD, a platform and set of sixteen analyses for revealing and comparing the
contents of large text corpora - general statistics, quality, social factors, and
inclusion of evaluation data (contamination) - built on two basic capabilities, count
and search, at scale, analyzing more than 35 TB across major corpora.

## Key findings
- WIMBD provides count and search at scale over large corpora (The Pile, C4,
  RedPajama, and others), exposing statistics, duplicates, benchmark contamination,
  and social-factor breakdowns (abstract).
- Peer-review: ICLR 2024 (per the 2026-09-03 verification in
  docs/next-investigations.md).

## Relevance to core question
The contamination-audit backbone for the thesis pilot (issue #3): exact-match and
n-gram search over The Pile (Pythia) and Dolma-adjacent corpora, with canary calibration
on top. Answers criterion 3's practical prerequisite - without corpus audit,
contamination cannot be excluded. Tooling; no reasoning claim.
