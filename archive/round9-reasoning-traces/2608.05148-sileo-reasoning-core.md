## Abstract (condensed)
Introduces Reasoning Core, a collection of 50 procedural generators for verifiable
reasoning problems spanning mathematics, logic, planning, state tracking, formal
languages, structured data, games, causality, and code, with semantic scorers, difficulty
controls, and task evaluators, studied as completion-supervised training data.

## Key findings
- 50 generators with difficulty controls and evaluators; compared with existing
  procedural suites (Procedural Warmup, Reasoning Gym, and others) under a matched
  completion-supervised protocol (abstract).
- Validates the procedural-generator approach on base models (includes OLMo-1B per the
  2026-09-03 verification in docs/next-investigations.md): generators produce useful,
  verifiable reasoning problems at scale.
- It is a pretraining-data/continued-training study, not a zero-shot held-out
  evaluation - the distinction the archive's H1 criteria turn on.

## Relevance to core question
Reusable infrastructure for the thesis study design (task families with difficulty
controls and verifiable answers). Supports feasibility of Condition 2 (procedural
generation with held-out seeds) but makes no out-of-the-box reasoning claim.
