## Abstract (condensed)
Existing test-time reasoning paradigms rely on externally imposed control (fixed
reasoning programs or costly expansion in constrained search spaces). State of Thought
(SoT) enables endogenous reasoning: the model's internal reasoning state governs how
reasoning unfolds. SoT extracts a compact dynamics-geometric state from the model's
internal information transfer and uses a 582-parameter controller on frozen backbones to
selectively activate historical reasoning support useful under the current state, framing
reasoning as a state-conditioned process over evidence rather than an externally
prescribed token chain. Gains: 1.34x quantitative, 1.62x general, 1.76x symbolic-and-code,
2.51x long-context across 3 LLMs and 16 datasets, while reducing generated tokens by
62.6% and end-to-end latency by 44.6%; on 2 VLM scales and 3 tasks, +3.8 points over
reasoning baselines with 74.9% fewer completion tokens and 73.5% lower latency. Under
constrained access, SoT retains 38.2%/36.5% mean accuracy gains in training-free/
embedding-only settings, and trajectory-only judging reaches 84.1% agreement across 3 API
models.

## Key findings (verified from full text)
- Endogenous, state-driven reasoning via a 582-parameter controller on frozen backbones.
- Consistent accuracy gains (1.34x-2.51x) with large token and latency reductions
  (62.6% fewer tokens, 44.6% lower latency).
- Retains 38.2%/36.5% mean gains in training-free / embedding-only settings.

## Relevance to core question
Assessment: H0-b-adjacent. "Endogenous" is the most in-weights-leaning framing in this
round, but the mechanism is a trained controller (582 parameters) selecting latent
support - still an elicitation scaffold on frozen backbones, not a native forward pass.
The training-free / embedding-only retention is the closest result in the round to a
capability already in the weights. Fails out-of-the-box (external controller; standard
benchmarks, not procedurally novel post-cutoff tasks). Meets none of the five acceptance
conditions. Overall verdict unchanged.
