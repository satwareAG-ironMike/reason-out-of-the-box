## Abstract (condensed)
Language models are deployed for general problem solving but are confined to token-level,
left-to-right decision-making during inference, falling short on tasks requiring
exploration, strategic lookahead, or where initial decisions are pivotal. Tree of
Thoughts (ToT) generalizes over Chain of Thought prompting to enable exploration over
coherent units of text ("thoughts") as intermediate steps. ToT allows deliberate decision
making: considering multiple reasoning paths and self-evaluating choices to decide the
next action, and looking ahead or backtracking when necessary for global choices. On
three novel tasks requiring non-trivial planning or search (Game of 24, Creative Writing,
Mini Crosswords), ToT significantly enhances problem-solving; on Game of 24, GPT-4 with
CoT prompting solved only 4% of tasks while ToT reached a 74% success rate.

## Key findings (verified from full text)
- Generalizes CoT into exploration over intermediate "thoughts" with self-evaluation,
  lookahead, and backtracking.
- Game of 24: GPT-4 + CoT 4% -> ToT 74% success; gains on Creative Writing and Mini
  Crosswords too.

## Relevance to core question
Assessment: the foundational deliberate-search scaffold for the latent-reasoning cluster
in this round; the explicit baseline BASIN (2609.00738) improves over. It is the
strongest in-weights contrast to "out of the box": the reasoning comes from an external
deliberate search procedure (candidate generation + self-evaluation + backtracking), not
from a native forward pass. It therefore opposes the out-of-the-box reading and is the
reference point against which training-free search methods (BASIN, DF-Sample) are
measured. Fails all five acceptance conditions (external scaffold; standard tasks).
Overall verdict unchanged.
