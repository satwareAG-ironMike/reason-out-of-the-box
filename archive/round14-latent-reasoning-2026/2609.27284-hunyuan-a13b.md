## Abstract (condensed)
Presents Hunyuan-A13B, an open-source Mixture-of-Experts LLM: 80 billion total
parameters, only 13 billion active at inference, balancing capability, efficiency, and
deployment cost. Pretrained on a rigorously filtered 20T-token corpus with enhanced STEM
data curation (improving factual reliability and reasoning), then high-quality SFT and
large-scale RL. Introduces a dual-mode Chain-of-Thought framework that adapts reasoning
depth to task complexity (fast thinking for routine queries, slow thinking for complex
multi-step problems). Competitive performance across mathematics, science, programming,
general language understanding, and agent tasks, often approaching much larger models,
with high inference throughput. Released to support open research and deployment.

## Key findings (from abstract)
- Open-source MoE model: 80B total / 13B active; 20T-token filtered corpus with enhanced
  STEM curation; SFT + large-scale RL.
- Dual-mode CoT (fast vs slow thinking) adapting reasoning depth to task complexity.

## Relevance to core question
Assessment: model-list / feasibility datapoint, not a reasoning result. Its value to the
repo is as a candidate for the open-data model list in the pre-registered study battery
(docs/study-design.md): an open MoE with an explicit dual-mode CoT and disclosed
training, suitable for the T1-T9 battery. It is not evidence for or against out-of-the-box
reasoning by itself (the reasoning capability here is explicitly SFT + RL elicited).
Meets none of the five acceptance conditions. Overall verdict unchanged.
