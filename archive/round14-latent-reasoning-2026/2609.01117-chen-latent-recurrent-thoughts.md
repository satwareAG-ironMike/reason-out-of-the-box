## Abstract (condensed)
CoT unfolds in discrete token space: each step is committed as text, errors propagate,
and eliciting good traces presupposes traces to imitate. Reasoning in a continuous
representation space sidesteps these constraints but leaves open how latent states should
be computed. This paper keeps a large LLM frozen (using it for what it is good at:
modeling and decoding sequences) while a small auxiliary network supplies continuous
latent thoughts, produced by recurrence: a tiny recurrent reasoner refines them over many
steps, decoupling the depth of computation from model size. Instantiated as Latent
Recurrent Thoughts (LRT): a task-dedicated proposer supplies base latents, a recurrent
reasoner refines them through bounded residual corrections, and the frozen LLM decodes
the answer. On symbolic reasoning with answer supervision but no reasoning traces
(Countdown-4, Sudoku) and on natural-language reasoning (HumanEval, MBPP, StrategyQA),
LRT substantially outperforms prior frozen-decoder continuous-space methods under an
identical decoder, prompt, data, and training budget, and outperforms non-thinking-mode
CoT prompting on the same backbone at a small fraction of its inference compute.

## Key findings (verified from full text)
- Decouples computation depth from model size: a small recurrent reasoner refines latent
  states; the large LLM stays frozen as decoder.
- Substantially outperforms prior frozen-decoder continuous-space methods under identical
  decoder/prompt/data/training budget; outperforms non-thinking-mode CoT prompting at a
  small fraction of its inference compute.

## Relevance to core question
Assessment: H0-b-adjacent latent-reasoning method. The frozen LLM decodes, but the
reasoning is delegated to a trained auxiliary recurrent reasoner - an elicitation
scaffold, not a native forward pass. The "no reasoning traces needed, answer
supervision only" property is notable (avoids the trace-imitation requirement of
verbal CoT) but the continuous thoughts come from a trained proposer + recurrent
refiner. Fails out-of-the-box (trained auxiliary; standard benchmark tasks). Meets none
of the five acceptance conditions. Overall verdict unchanged.
