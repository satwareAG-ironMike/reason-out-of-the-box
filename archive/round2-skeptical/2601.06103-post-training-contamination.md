## Abstract (condensed)
Controlled study: inject 5 copies of GSM8K/MBPP test items into an otherwise-clean 25B-token
extended pretraining run of Qwen2.5 (0.5B/1.5B) and Gemma3 (1B/4B), then compare clean vs
contaminated models across pretraining, SFT, and GRPO (RL) post-training.

## Key findings (verified from full text)
- Continued clean pretraining can drive the apparent contamination gap to nearly zero -
  the leaked signal is **submerged, not erased**, and is "readily rediscovered during
  post-training."
- **SFT** inflates scores only on the contaminated benchmark (pure extraction of leakage).
- **GRPO (RL)** widens the contaminated-model gap but *also* produces gains on
  **uncontaminated** benchmarks - RL converts leaked data into more transferable capability.
- Scale amplifies the divergence: SFT over-estimation grows with size; GRPO's
  contamination-gap actually shrinks (even negative) with size.

## Relevance to core question
**Highly relevant 2026 nuance.** Disentangles "memorization" from "RL-amplified capability":
RL-based reasoning training genuinely generalizes beyond the leaked items, which weakens the
pure-memorization skeptic story for RL-trained models while confirming SFT-era benchmark
inflation. Preprint: treat with care until reviewed.
