## Abstract

Complete, proofread and annotated synapse-resolution connectome of the entire male
Drosophila melanogaster central nervous system: central brain, both optic lobes and ventral
nerve cord (~166,700 neurons; ~11,710 neuron types), reconstructed from a single FIB-SEM
volume at 8 nm resolution by HHMI Janelia FlyEM with Google Research (AI-assisted
segmentation incl. flood-filling networks and PATHFINDER), Cambridge Connectomics Group and
MRC LMB. Dataset MaleCNS v1.0 released earlier at male-cns.janelia.org (CC-BY 4.0;
preprint bioRxiv 10.1101/2025.10.09.680999). Together with the female connectome
(Nature 10.1038/s41586-026-10735-w) it completes the first male/female pair for one species.
Abstract per PubMed record plus cross-checked primary reporting (Google Research blog,
HHMI news release); not full-text verified.

## Key findings

- Sex comparison: 8,069 isomorphic neuron types (broadly corresponding organization in both
  sexes), 138 sexually dimorphic types, 289 male-specific types, 71 female-specific types.
- Dimorphism concentrates in higher-order brain centers; sensory and motor periphery is
  largely shared between males and females.
- Male-specific connections form organized connectivity hotspots; dimorphic neurons can
  reroute information between otherwise shared circuits - a wiring-level mechanism by which
  equal sensory input can drive different male/female behavior.
- Largest brain map by neuron count to date; ~95% of cells shared between sexes at the type
  level (secondary reporting).
- Companion papers same day: visual system (Cell 10.1016/j.cell.2026.08.014), taste
  (10.1016/j.cell.2026.08.016), social behavior (Current Biology 10.1016/j.cub.2026.08.013).

## Relevance to core question

Context/analogy evidence only - this is connectomics background for counterarguments #9 and
#6, not LLM-reasoning evidence; it does not change the verdict. It grounds what a real
nervous system looks like at wiring resolution and what a connectome does NOT specify:
neuron dynamics, learning rules, neuromodulation, sensory transduction or motor semantics.
The September 2026 hobbyist simulation wave over this dataset is a live public case of
scaffold-vs-model attribution (#6): behavior required developer-engineered sensory
encoding (e.g., game frames -> photoreceptor neurons), motor decoding (descending neurons ->
game controls) and objectives around static wiring; no demo showed behavior from wiring
alone. See docs/next-investigations.md research findings 2026-09-14 for the full event and
fidelity analysis.
