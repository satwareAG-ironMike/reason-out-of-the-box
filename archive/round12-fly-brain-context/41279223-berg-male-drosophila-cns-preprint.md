## Abstract

Preprint version of the male Drosophila central nervous system connectome paper, first
posted 2025-10-09 (v2 2025-10-30), PubMed-indexed at the preprint record PMID 41279223.
Superseded by the peer-reviewed Cell version (PMID 42691995, published 2026-09-03), whose
final counts differ from the preprint - cite the Cell version for figures.

## Key findings

- Same study as the Cell entry (PMID 42691995); the preprint contains earlier,
  preliminary neuron/synapse counts that differ from the final publication.
- Released alongside MaleCNS v1.0 dataset activity (dataset publicly downloadable at
  male-cns.janelia.org).
- Retained here as the citable record of the version-of-record history and of the
  June-to-September 2026 data/publication gap.

## Relevance to core question

Same context role as the Cell entry: connectomics background for counterarguments #9 and
#6, not LLM-reasoning evidence. The preprint-vs-Cell count difference is itself a small
case study in version-of-record discipline: figures should be cited from the peer-reviewed
version, and the thesis repository applies the same rule to its own freeze (issue #11).
