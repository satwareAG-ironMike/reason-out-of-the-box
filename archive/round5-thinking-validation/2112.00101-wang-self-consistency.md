## Abstract (condensed)
Replaces greedy CoT decoding with sampling multiple diverse reasoning paths and taking the
majority final answer. Large gains on GSM8K (+17.9%), SVAMP (+11.0%), AQuA (+12.2%) over
CoT with greedy decoding.

## Key findings
- Validation by **consensus**: if several independent reasoning trajectories reach the same
  answer, it is more likely correct. No external verifier needed.
- Works only where answers are comparable (exact-match style); open-ended tasks lack a
  voteable equivalence class.
- Mechanistically: correct reasoning paths converge on the same answer; erroneous paths
  diverge - agreement as error filter.

## Relevance to core question
**The cheapest working validation scheme, and its limits.** Consistency is a statistical
validator over the model's own distribution - correlated errors (shared misconceptions,
contamination) pass the vote. It validates against the model's prior, not against the world.
