## Abstract (condensed)
Trains a single model to simultaneously generate step-by-step reasoning and evaluate the
correctness of its own outputs at inference time, without external feedback: self-rewarding
correction combining QW (self-critique) with iterative DPO-style preference training, plus a
learned stopping rule for refinement loops.

## Key findings
- Validation can be *trained into* the same model: a learned verifier head that scores its
  own reasoning chains, improving self-correction on MATH500/AIME-style tasks.
- The reward signal still originates from ground-truth labels during training - what the
  model gains is an internalized critic, not ungrounded introspection.
- Includes learned termination: deciding *when to stop* revising is part of validation.

## Relevance to core question
**2025 state of the art: validation moves from prompting (2023) to training (2025).** The
verifier is distilled from external ground truth, then reused internally - closing part of
the gap Huang identified, but confirming that the ability is manufactured, not innate.
