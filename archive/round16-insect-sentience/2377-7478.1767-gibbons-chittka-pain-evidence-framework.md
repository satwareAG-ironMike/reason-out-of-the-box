## Abstract

Condensed from the publisher abstract (Crossref/OpenAlex records). Abstract-level
verification, not full-text verified. Crump et al. define eight criteria indicating sentience
in animals, with a focus on pain. This paper points out the risk of false-negative or
false-positive diagnoses of pain.

## Key findings

- Criteria at different levels of inclusivity serve two purposes: applying the precautionary
  principle in animal welfare considerations, and more formal scientific evidence of pain.
- The authors suggest tightening the criteria - from more general evidence of sentience to
  pain alone - because crucial evidence for animal welfare decisions might otherwise be
  missed for animals subjected to invasive and injurious procedures.
