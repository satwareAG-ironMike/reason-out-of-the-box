## Abstract

Condensed from the publisher highlights (Crossref/OpenAlex records). Abstract-level
verification, not full-text verified. Response to the reassessment (entry 12): examines the
revised models and new data on motivational trade-offs in bees.

## Key findings

- The revised models are overfit, and there is no evidence of the negative interaction the
  trade-off argument requires.
- The authors recommend better experimental designs and clearly aversive stimuli for testing
  motivational trade-offs.

## Relevance to core question

The bee trade-off controversy is unresolved: both sides publish peer-reviewed re-analyses of
the same data. The claim ledger records this as a live disagreement, not a settled fact
(docs/claims-insect-sentience.md). Not reasoning evidence; overall verdict unchanged.
