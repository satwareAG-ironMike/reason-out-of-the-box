## Abstract (condensed)
Studies whether transformers can learn implicit reasoning over parametric knowledge
(composition and comparison) without CoT. Finds they can - but only via grokking (extended
training far beyond overfitting). Generalization differs by reasoning type: composition fails
to systematically generalize OOD; comparison succeeds. Provides mechanistic analysis of why.

## Key findings (verified from full text)
- Implicit (single-pass, no-CoT) multi-hop reasoning *can* form, but requires extreme
  training and then still does not compose systematically (relation composition fails OOD).
- Mechanistic story: the circuit handles known relation pairs; genuinely novel chains are
  not executed, only previously seen patterns are replayed.

## Relevance to core question
**Direct empirical bridge between Faith-and-Fate skepticism and mechanistic analysis**: even
when a model demonstrably implements implicit reasoning circuits, the circuits generalize
only along trained axes. Peer-reviewed support for the trained-depth ceiling.
