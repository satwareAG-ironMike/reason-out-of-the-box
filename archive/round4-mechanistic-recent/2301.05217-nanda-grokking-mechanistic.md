## Abstract (condensed)
Uses mechanistic interpretability to find continuous progress measures underlying grokking on
modular addition: fully reverse-engineers a small transformer into a human-understandable
algorithm - circular representations of inputs, trigonometric angle-addition identities in a
rotation matrix, and a cleanup of memorizing components as the generalizing circuit forms.

## Key findings (verified from full text)
- The generalizing network implements a genuine **discrete Fourier-space arithmetic
  algorithm** - verified causally by ablation, not inferred from behavior.
- Grokking is the observable surface of a continuous circuit-formation process: emergence
  looks discontinuous only at the behavioral level.

## Relevance to core question
**The mechanistic existence proof**: transformers trained by gradient descent on examples
implement real algorithms, not lookup tables. This is the strongest available answer to "is
it all statistics?" - but it was demonstrated on a toy task with explicit training examples,
not in a pretrained LLM doing reasoning out of the box.
