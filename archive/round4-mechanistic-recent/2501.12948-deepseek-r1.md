## Abstract (condensed)
Introduces DeepSeek-R1-Zero: RL (GRPO) with rule-based rewards directly on DeepSeek-V3-Base,
no supervised CoT data. Reports AIME 2024 pass@1 rising ~15.6% -> 71.0% over training,
emergence of reflection, verification, and long CoT. R1 adds cold-start SFT for readability.

## Key findings
- Verifiable-reward RL alone can elicit sustained reasoning trajectories from a base model.
- Reported Aha moment: model spontaneously reconsiders early approaches mid-trajectory.
- The trajectory from 15.6% to 71% quantifies what pure RL adds on top of the base model -
  but note the base model's nonzero 15.6% starting point on olympiad problems.

## Relevance to core question
**Defines the modern "reasoning models" paradigm and quantifies the elicitation gap.** The
paper itself never claims the base model reasons out of the box; the cited follow-up
(COLM 2025) shows even the 15.6% start and the Aha behavior were partly pre-existing. Nature
publication makes it the most authoritative statement of the RL-elicitation position.
