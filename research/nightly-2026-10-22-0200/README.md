# Round 17 (2026-10-22 02:00): the fixed-budget depth curve to k = 48

Tracker: issue #65. Branch `research/nightly-2026-10-22-0200` (stacked on the
round-16 branch, so the round-15 archive is in-tree as the anchor). Endpoint: a
flash-next-class local endpoint (the class round 15 measured at k = 12, 16, 24;

## Question

Round 6 established that the repaired ladders family is budget-limited, not
depth-limited, on this endpoint class: completion length grows ~linearly with
k, the answer word is emitted only after the traversal, and a fixed budget
deletes answers rather than reasoning; the recorded consequence
(`docs/next-investigations.md`, 2026-10-16) is that a depth claim must be taken
at a fixed per-item budget. Round 15 ran the matched-pair grid at k = 12, 16,
24 at `max_tokens = 2048`: 36 of 36 answered cells correct, six of twelve cells
at k = 24 truncated with the answer rescued from the reasoning trace. Two
questions, one curve: **does answerability turn over by k = 48 at the fixed
2048-token budget, and if so, is the turnover an answerability (budget)
collapse or a correctness collapse?**

Predictions were declared in the gate (`gate.json`, schema `round17-gate-v1`,
gate_hash `5e6ce0d6e7975c52d364aa7edcbecd3adecb306fcad537e83a6264c2627305f0`)
before any prompt was spent:

- **P1** every answered cell correct (budget-limited turnover);
- **P2** answerability non-increasing in k with a strict drop at 32 or 48, F1
  answering at least as often as X at the deepest truncated level;
- **P3** truncated cells die late (median expected-traversal coverage >= 0.8).

## Answer

**No turnover within the declared grid.** 36 of 36 cells answered, 36 of 36
correct (per level 12/12, Wilson 95% lower bound 0.758 per level; per arm
F1/R1/F2/X each 9/9). The directed-traversal oracle matches all 36 cells;
undirected connectivity matches 27 (failing exactly the nine R1 cells); exact
McNemar shows zero discordant pairs in both matched families (p = 1.0). The
(family, endpoint-class) depth ceiling is not inside k = 48 at this budget.

Predictions: **P1 holds** (every answered cell correct); **P2 fails honestly**
- answerability did not decay (1.00 / 1.00 / 1.00 at k = 24 / 32 / 48);
**P3 is vacuous** - zero `truncated_no_answer` cells, so late-death coverage
could not be computed. The round's information is where the budget pressure
went instead:

- **The ceiling was hit constantly and the answer survived anyway.** 17 of 36
  cells finished with `finish_reason = length` (levels 24/32/48: 6 / 4 / 7);
  every one carried a parseable yes/no inside the reasoning trace. The
  X arm is extreme: 9 of 9 X cells hit 2048 at all three levels (arm mean
  completion 2048.0), 0 answers in `content`; R1: 7 of 9 via the reasoning
  fallback. Without the declared parser's reasoning fallback this run would
  score 17 of 36 answered and 0 of 9 on X - the rescue convention is
  load-bearing, and its hazard is disclosed below.
- **Mid-stream commitment:** across the 17 ceiling completions the median
  yes/no sits at 0.457 of the reasoning stream (max 0.9996): the model commits
  to its answer around mid-traversal and keeps generating afterwards. The
  answer is not, on this class, the last thing the model computes.
- **Positive arms get cheap-early:** among answered F1/F2 cells with a natural
  stop, completion tokens fit `-50.8 + 33.1 * k` (Pearson r = 0.845) - well
  under the ~80 tokens/hop open-budget law of round 6 - so at k = 48 the
  positive arms answer correctly without writing the full traversal.
  Full-chain recitation (all interior nodes named) is 9 of 36 cells (round 15:
  19/36; round 16: 5/36): the class recites less as depth grows and stays
  correct.
- **Anchor rerun (cell-paired to round 15, same class):** 12 of 12 answers
  agree, outcomes identical, completion tokens equal or near-equal (e.g. 609
  vs 609, 2048 vs 2048, 720 vs 761). Temperature-0 rerun reproducibility on
  this class is confirmed at the answer level and approximate at the token
  level.
- Level means are non-monotone (mean completion 1370 / 1317 / 1822; latency
  driven by the 2048-cap mix), because per-item chain counts vary
  (`n_chains` is seeded per item) and the char cap reshuffles which deep items
  enter the grid; depth and prompt load remain coupled (issue #71 option-2
  decoupling is still open).

## Adjacent literature (abstract-level, arXiv API, 2026-10-22)

The general phenomenon is not unoccupied. arXiv:2605.07686 (Nie et al., 2026,
preprint) names the shared-budget mechanism the **coupling tax**: when
reasoning traces and final answers share one output-token budget, long traces
crowd out the answer, measured on GSM8K/MATH-500/BBH with Qwen3 models at
budgets up to 2048 - the same mechanism this round localizes per arm on a
reachability task with an oracle-known gold. arXiv:2607.21433 (Oladri et al.,
2026, preprint) characterizes the bimodal converged/non-converged split under
budget exhaustion (90.3% vs 6.6% accuracy on AIME) - the correctness-collapse
side this grid did not reach. Budget forcing itself (arXiv:2501.19393, s1) and
budget-aware allocation (arXiv:2412.18547) are the control levers. The
specific measurement here (matched-pair k-hop transitivity grid, per-arm
budget allocation and mid-stream commitment at a fixed 2048 budget,
cell-paired cross-run anchors) was not found in the scanned records. The
first literature pass of the round failed outright (6/6 calls, arXiv 429/503
and one OpenAlex 400, zero records; retry notes recorded in the local learning
loop), and the second pass used the HTTPS arXiv endpoint (the HTTP endpoint
returned empty bodies, the same failure the repo logged on 2026-09-11); with a
12-call cap the scan is a frame, not a systematic review, and no novelty claim
rests on it.

## Disclosure and limits

No independent verifier pass: state **Executed**, not Reproduced. n = 9 per
arm and n = 3 items per level: exploratory, not confirmatory. The reasoning
fallback parser scores cells the endpoint never surfaced in `content`; the
rescued token is the LAST yes/no in a still-running trace, which is a
convention, not a transcript of the model's final word (arXiv:2605.07686 is
the adjacent warning; round 16 showed the rescue property does not transfer
across classes). The rerun anchor pins one endpoint of one class: results
remain one endpoint per class, quantized local deployment, undisclosed
training data; not H1 evidence. Prompt load and depth stay coupled at these
levels (#71 option 2 open). Levels 32/48 have no cell-paired prior.
