## Why

The program holds two readings of the same wall and has never had a channel that separates them.
Round 59 (a flash-next-class build, `research/nightly-2026-11-11-0318/`) recorded delivery never
breaking: 14 of 17 name cells correct and every failure at `j >= 40` a name on the right path at
the wrong step (asked step 40, answered step 38; asked step 48, answered step 1). Round 63
(a 27B-class build, `research/nightly-2026-11-13-0348/`) recorded three of four endpoint cells
censored at the 2,048 cap with the gold terminal inside the trace. Both are reported as "the wall
is at depth", and they say opposite things about what is missing: round 59's model may hold a
completed walk and mislabel its index, while round 63's may hold the walk and lose the channel that
reports it. Issue #71 asks which resource is scarce, issue #126 asks whether the wall is an output
artifact, issue #129 owns the `k > 48` name panel.

The instrument for that split is the oldest one in memory research: a recognition test over the
store a recall test failed (recognition without cued recall, DOI:10.1080/09658211.2024.2397037;
the formal version is the knowledge-prediction gap, arXiv:2509.23782). None of the 63 rounds before
this one ran one: every depth number in this repository is free-response recall.

## Method

One item, one asked step `j`, two ways to answer, each its own stateless single-turn request.

- **Recall arm**, the archived name-arm template. Four of its prompts reproduce archived records
  byte-wise by prompt sha256 (the `k = 48` anchor at `j = 8` and `j = 48`, the `k = 64` item at
  `j = 64` and at its dead-end ask, verified by the `L1` row), so the recall arm is a cross-firing
  duplicate of rounds 51 through 54 and round 63 and not a new task.
- **Recognition arm**, the same item and the same `j` with five options: the gold, three
  distractors and the word `NONE`. Distractors come only from that item's own vocabulary, adjacent
  step positions first (`j - 1`, `j + 1`, `j - 2`), because adjacent steps are exactly where the
  archived errors landed. Nothing outside the item is ever offered, so no option can be recognised
  from a prior round. Chance is one in five.

The arms are a minimal pair: the rule line, the fact block and the task sentence are byte
identical, and only the answer clause differs (`C5`). Each dispatch is a single-turn request with
no session, so the practice effect a recall attempt has on a later recognition test in human work
(Wenger et al. 1980, DOI:10.1037/0278-7393.6.2.135) cannot operate here: the two arms are
independent samples of one latent state, not a test-retest pair.

Items are the load-floor family (`facts = 2k`) generated by `generators/ladders.py` at seed base
20261005: the archived `k = 48` anchor (yes/no digest `068bc6a1fb0620fb`, recertified locally
before any call), the first load-floor item at `k = 40`, and the first load-floor item at `k = 64`,
which is round 63's item.

Controls follow the issue #139 fix: every NONE control asks for one step from a path tail, so a
control cannot inherit a deep cell's output budget, which is what made rounds 55, 59 and 60 die on
their own controls gate. Dispatch order is part of the registration: the four recognition form
cells run first, and the run stops there if the recognition instrument fails them, so a broken
instrument cannot spend the round.

## Registered decision table

Frozen in `gate.json` before the first dispatch. Tier order: `form_invalid` >
`dispatch_incomplete` > `controls_invalid` > `no_dissociation_material` > `readout_failure` /
`representation_failure` / `mixed`.

| Id | Row | Threshold |
|----|-----|-----------|
| `F1` | recognition delivers the gold on both shallow cells and picks `NONE` on both dead-end controls | 4/4 |
| `F2` | recall delivers the gold on both shallow cells and `NONE` on both dead-end controls | 4/4 |

| `D2` | among those missed cells the recognition pick is counted as gold, the same wrong name recall declared, another option, `NONE`, off-options, or no delivery | descriptive |

Theory rows `T1` to `T5` in `gate.json` carry the external records the design stands on: option
position bias (arXiv:2309.03882, arXiv:2308.11483), format is not neutral (arXiv:2507.02856,
arXiv:2503.14996, arXiv:2503.13508), the recall and recognition dissociation
(DOI:10.1080/09658211.2024.2397037, DOI:10.1037/0278-7393.6.2.135), latent knowledge versus
emission and unfaithful traces (arXiv:2509.23782, arXiv:2207.05221, arXiv:2305.04388), and step
counting as a capacity separate from graph connectivity (arXiv:2305.18654, arXiv:2407.15160,
arXiv:2405.18719). Every one of them is abstract-level or metadata-level verification in this
firing and none is quoted.

## Findings

1. **Where both channels could answer, they split one cell each way.** On the `k = 64` item at
   `j = 48` recall censored and recognition delivered the correct terminal at 1,602 tokens; on the
   `k = 40` item at `j = 40` recall delivered the correct terminal at 1,779 tokens and recognition
   censored at 2,048. Constraining the answer space therefore neither reliably saves nor reliably
   costs the budget: the two channels are not ordered by difficulty, and at one cell per direction
   neither flip is a result. What the pair does establish is that the wall crossing point sits in
   the completion length and not in the asked step, since the `k = 40` item delivers its own
   terminal at `j = 40` while the `k = 48` anchor censors at `j = 40`, and no `j` band separates
   delivered from censored records.
2. **The registered tier is decided by censoring, not by evidence about representation.** 4 of the
   5 recall-missed paired cells were recall non-deliveries, and on all 4 the recognition arm also
   censored at exactly 2,048 (`D2` no delivery 4), so it never expressed a choice. Counting a
   censored arm as a failed recognition produced `representation_failure` from records that only
   say both channels ran out of budget. A contrast row of this shape needs a delivery-conditioned
   denominator, which is the durable gap this round files.
3. **Zero wrong answers in 12 deliveries, and the wrong-step signature is absent on this build.**
   The error census finds no `near_step_offset`, no far-on-path and no off-item name anywhere,
   which replicates round 50 ("accuracy never turns over, only delivery does") and contradicts the
   round-59 shape on the same class. The build fingerprint ties this firing to the serving identity
   rounds 55 and 60 recorded and excludes round 59's build, so the reading is identity-tied: the
   build that stops itself at about 1,557 tokens emits wrong positions, the build that runs to the
   cap emits nothing. That is one firing per identity, so it is a reading and not a result, and it
   turns the round-59 wrong-step finding into a prediction about the stopping build: there a wrong
   position should coincide with a self-truncated trace, and the emitted name should be the last
   position its own trace reached.
4. **The issue #139 controls fix works, and this is the first deep-ladder firing in this program
   whose controls row certified with controls as the only shallow-shape arm.** Rounds 55, 59 and 60
   all scored `controls_invalid`; here `F1` and `F2` are both 4/4 with the one-step path-tail
   control shape as the only control shape in the panel, and the controls are cheap: 145 and 152
   completion tokens on the `k = 64` item and 1,016 and 1,002 on the `k = 48` anchor, where round
   60's off-end controls censored at exactly 2,048. No control named an entity in either channel.
5. **The premise of issue #126 does not hold on this firing.** That issue records the 2,048
   truncation wall as collapsed to zero on both classes; here it is 11 of 23 records at exactly
   2,048 with an empty answer channel on a flash-next-class endpoint. Whether the wall is present
   is a property of the endpoint and its load and has to be measured per firing (this round
   measured it at the calibration cell, where the answer arrived at 1,176 tokens), not assumed.
6. **Issue #105 census:** `reasoning_content` on 23 of 23 records. This is a fourth same-class
   firing with the channel exposed (rounds 55, 59, 60 and 64), while the 27B class has shown both
   channels across firings (`reasoning_content` on round 61, `reasoning` on round 63), so exposure
   stays a deployment property rather than a class property. This round claims nothing from trace
   content: rounds 50 and 60 established that on this task the trace restates the fact list, which
   sets the provenance gap to 0.00 and makes such a row inadmissible.
7. **Issue #100 self-agreement is vacuous on this firing, recorded so it is not read as a pass.**
   The byte-identical duplicate pair both censored at 2,048 with an empty answer channel.
8. **Rate hygiene (issue #141):** the measured pre-gate rate predicted the round (649.6 s of record
   walls against a 1,800 s worst-case budget), but per-call throughput inside one healthy firing
   varied more than 4-fold, 20.7 tokens per second on the slowest cell against a 25.0 s median,
   with no cell dropped or retried.

## Limits

- One endpoint, one firing, one build. `n` is 1 per cell: the primary row is a paired contrast, not
  a distribution, and the binomial is over cells within one firing across three items, so its
  `p` value is descriptive.
- The recognition arm is not a format-neutral re-asking of the same task. Four in-item candidates
  give the model four anchors to verify against, and the multiple-choice versus generative
  literature (arXiv:2507.02856, arXiv:2503.13508, arXiv:2503.14996) shows a recognition gain can be
  format inflation rather than competence. A recognition win here licenses "reachable under this
  response format", not "the model knows it".
- Option-position bias is recorded (`C4`) and not tested: at this `n` a slot test has no power, the
  keys are spread rather than balanced by design, and all delivered picks were correct, which is
  the case in which a slot test can say nothing. A follow-up needs a per-cell key permutation, not
  one shuffle.
- The answer clauses differ in length between the arms (the recognition clause is longer), so the
  minimal pair is byte-identical through the task sentence only and a clause-length confound cannot
  be excluded from this design. It is recorded, not argued away.
- Distractors are adjacent step positions of the same walk, which makes the recognition arm
  strictly harder than a random-distractor control would. No random-distractor arm was run, so no
  absolute recognition score is interpretable, only the paired contrast against the recall arm.
- Because 11 of 23 records censored, this round answers its registered question far less sharply
  than its tier label suggests. The honest summary is the paired table above, not the tier.
